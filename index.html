Find answers to the most common queries about CCTV & Biometric installations in Surat.

How Many CCTV Cameras Does Your Surat Business Need?

The right camera count depends on your premises size and layout. For a typical 500 sq ft shop in Surat's commercial areas like Ring Road, Udhna, or Katargam, 4 cameras are ideal — entrance, cash counter, and two aisle views. For textile units and factories, we recommend one camera per 400–500 sq ft of floor area plus full perimeter coverage. Our team offers a free site survey to recommend the exact count for your location.

Fingerprint vs Face Recognition — Which is Right for You?

Fingerprint machines are cost-effective and ideal for offices up to 500 employees. Face recognition systems are better for large factories, environments where workers wear gloves (like Surat diamond units and textile mills), and post-COVID hygiene needs. Both integrate with payroll software. eSSL and ZKTeco are our top-selling brands for Surat businesses. Contact us for a free demo at your location.

Best Night Vision CCTV Cameras for Businesses (2025)

1. Hikvision DS-2CE5AD0T-IRPF – Best overall, 2MP, 30m IR, indoor turret. 2. CP Plus 2MP Dome – Good value for shops, 20m IR. 3. Dahua HAC-T1A21 – ColorVu full color night vision, no IR needed. 4. CP Plus Bullet 2MP – Outdoor use, IP67, 40m range. All available at Acme Infotech with installation support.

How to View CCTV on Your Mobile from Anywhere

For Hikvision: Download Hik-Connect app → create account → scan QR code from DVR menu → device added. For CP Plus: Use CamView app. For Dahua: Use DMSS app. You need a minimum 2 Mbps upload speed at the camera location. Our Acme Infotech technicians configure remote viewing on your phone during installation at no extra charge. Works on Jio, Airtel, and BSNL.

How Biometric Attendance Saves Surat Businesses Time & Money

A Surat textile unit with 80 workers was losing hundreds of hours through proxy attendance (buddy punching). After installing an eSSL biometric machine, attendance accuracy reached 99.9%. Payroll errors dropped to zero and the HR team saved 8 hours monthly. ROI achieved in under 3 weeks. Contact Acme Infotech for a free demo.

Best CCTV Setup for Surat Homes & Societies

For bungalows in Vesu, Adajan, Pal, or Althan: Priority cameras are at the main gate, boundary corners, back door, and parking. For apartments and societies: Main lobby entrance, lift area, parking gate, and individual flat door. Wireless IP cameras work great for apartments — no drilling into walls. A complete home setup in Surat is very affordable. Free site visit available — call 90339 99401.
