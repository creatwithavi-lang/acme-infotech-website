Best CCTV Setup for Surat Homes, Bungalows and Societies

A home CCTV camera setup Surat families can depend on should cover entry points, parking, staircases, gates, and daily movement without invading privacy.

Published: Dec 20245 min readBy Acme Infotech Security System

Home security planning is different from business CCTV. The goal is peace of mind, visitor visibility, parking safety, and quick mobile access when family members are away.

  • Main gate and visitor entry.

Best camera points for societies

Societies should prioritize main gate, lobby, lift area, parking gate, basement, staircase access, and common passages. Camera placement should respect resident privacy while covering common movement.

Wired or wireless CCTV?

Wired systems are better for stable long-term recording. Wireless IP cameras are useful for apartments where drilling is limited. For full recording and multi-camera reliability, DVR or NVR systems are usually stronger.

Always test night view, mobile app access, and playback before final handover.
