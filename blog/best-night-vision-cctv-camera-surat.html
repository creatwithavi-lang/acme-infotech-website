Best Night Vision CCTV Cameras for Complete Security

A night vision CCTV camera Surat setup should be selected according to light level, distance, outdoor exposure, and the evidence detail you need after dark.

Published: Mar 20256 min readBy Acme Infotech Security System

Night security is one of the main reasons businesses install CCTV. But not every night vision camera works the same. Some use infrared black-and-white footage, some use full-color low-light technology, and some need supporting light to perform well.

Night vision camera types

TypeBest useOutput
IR cameraDark shops, passages, storesBlack-and-white night footage
Color night visionGates, parking, high-value areasColor footage in low light
Outdoor bulletPerimeter and vehicle movementLonger range coverage

IR night vision cameras

IR cameras are the most common choice for shops, passages, offices, and society entrances. They switch to black-and-white recording at night and are reliable when the area is very dark.

Color night vision cameras

ColorVu and similar cameras record color footage in low light. They are useful for vehicle color, clothing color, and better visual evidence. They perform best when some ambient light is available.

Dome, turret, or bullet?

  • Dome cameras are neat for indoor shops and offices.
  • Turret cameras are flexible and reduce reflection issues.
  • Bullet cameras are strong for outdoor gates, parking, and perimeter coverage.
For outdoor Surat conditions, check IP rating, night vision distance, lens angle, and warranty before buying.
