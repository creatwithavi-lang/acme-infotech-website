How Many CCTV Cameras Does Your Surat Business Need?

Planning CCTV camera installation in Surat becomes easier when you map entrances, cash areas, blind spots, storage zones, and outdoor approach points before choosing camera quantity.

Published: May 20255 min readBy Acme Infotech Security System

There is no single camera count that works for every business. A small shop in Katargam may need four cameras, while a textile unit in Ring Road or Udhna may need sixteen or more. The right CCTV camera installation Surat plan depends on the size of the site, number of entry points, lighting, and the areas where loss or disputes are most likely.

  • Small retail shop up to 500 sq ft: 4 cameras for entrance, billing counter, display area, and storage.
  • Office up to 1,000 sq ft: 6 to 8 cameras for reception, work area, server area, entry, and passage.
  • Warehouse or textile unit: 1 camera per 400 to 500 sq ft plus perimeter and loading points.
  • Diamond or jewellery office: cameras at entry, visitor seating, work tables, safe area, and exit.

Camera placement matters more than raw quantity

A professional CCTV camera dealer in Surat will first study blind spots. One well-positioned camera can cover more useful evidence than two cameras placed at the wrong angle. Priority should go to faces at entrance level, transactions at counters, stock movement, and vehicle access.

For most Surat businesses, a free site survey is the safest way to finalize camera quantity, DVR/NVR channels, hard disk capacity, and mobile viewing setup.

Do you need 2MP, 5MP, or 4K cameras?

For general indoor monitoring, 2MP cameras are often enough. For long passages, outdoor gates, number plates, and high-value inventory areas, 5MP or 4K cameras give better detail. Camera resolution should be matched with lens angle, night vision range, and recording storage.
