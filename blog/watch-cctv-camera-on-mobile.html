How to Watch Your CCTV Camera on Mobile from Anywhere

A correct CCTV camera mobile viewing setup lets owners check live video, playback, alerts, and recordings from outside the shop or home.

Published: Feb 20253 min readBy Acme Infotech Security System

Mobile viewing is one of the most requested CCTV features. Whether you use Hikvision, CP Plus, or Dahua, the setup requires a working internet connection, correct DVR/NVR settings, and the official mobile app.

Basic requirements

RequirementWhy it matters
DVR/NVR internetConnects the recorder to the mobile app cloud service.
Official appHik-Connect, DMSS, or CP Plus app gives secure access.
Upload speedImproves live view and playback outside the location.
  • DVR, NVR, or IP camera connected to internet.
  • Stable upload speed at the camera location.
  • Official app such as Hik-Connect, DMSS, or CP Plus app.
  • QR code or device serial number from the recorder.

Setup steps

  1. Create an account in the camera brand app.
  2. Enable platform access or P2P from DVR/NVR settings.
  3. Scan the QR code from the device menu.
  4. Set live view and playback permissions.
  5. Test viewing on mobile data, not only shop Wi-Fi.
